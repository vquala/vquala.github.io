About VQualA 2026

VQualA 2026: Human- and Machine-Aligned Visual Quality Assessment Challenge aims to advance reliable, interpretable, and human- and machine-aligned visual quality intelligence for next-generation AI systems.

Traditional image and video quality assessment methods have primarily been designed to characterize the perceptual experience of human observers. However, modern AI systems — including embodied agents, autonomous systems, multimodal foundation models, and large vision-language models — have become major consumers of visual data. These systems may exhibit perceptual sensitivities that differ substantially from those of human observers.

As a result, visual degradations that have limited impact on human perception may significantly affect downstream machine reasoning, navigation, recognition, generation, or decision-making. At the same time, emerging multimodal AI systems increasingly need to understand and reason about visual quality over complex and long-duration visual content.

VQualA 2026 therefore explores a new generation of visual quality assessment paradigms that jointly consider human perception, machine perception, and multimodal reasoning.

The challenge is organized under the IEEE Signal Processing Society Challenge Program and promotes reproducible benchmarking, publicly accessible datasets, standardized evaluation protocols, and open scientific dissemination.

Challenge Tracks

VQualA 2026 consists of two complementary tracks addressing emerging challenges in machine-centric perception and multimodal visual quality understanding.

Track 1 — MPD: Machine-Centric Perceptual Quality Assessment

Machine intelligence systems increasingly serve as primary consumers of visual data. However, traditional human-oriented image quality assessment methods cannot fully characterize how visual degradations influence machine perception and downstream intelligent tasks.

The MPD (Machine Preference Dataset) track focuses on machine-centric visual perception, embodied AI sensitivity, and downstream task-aware quality evaluation. Participants will develop methods capable of predicting multi-dimensional quality preferences aligned with machine intelligence requirements.

Key topics include:

  • Machine-centric perceptual quality assessment
  • Task-aware visual quality evaluation
  • Quality assessment for embodied AI
  • Human-machine perceptual alignment
  • Multi-dimensional quality prediction

Dataset and resources: MPD

Track 2 — LongVQU-Eval: Long-Video Quality Understanding and Reasoning

The LongVQU-Eval track investigates the ability of large vision-language models to understand, reason about, and explain perceptual quality degradations across long-duration videos.

Unlike conventional video quality assessment, this track requires models to reason across extended temporal contexts and identify when, where, and why perceptual quality changes occur.

Participants will address challenges including temporal distortion localization, cross-event quality reasoning, distortion attribution, and holistic long-video perceptual understanding.

Key topics include:

  • Long-context video quality understanding
  • Temporal quality reasoning
  • Distortion localization and attribution
  • Large vision-language models for quality assessment
  • Interpretable perceptual quality reasoning

Dataset and resources: LongVQU-Eval

Participation

VQualA 2026 is open to researchers, students, and practitioners worldwide. Participants may join individually or as teams.

The challenge will be hosted through IEEE DataPort, with supplementary GitHub repositories and benchmark webpages providing datasets, baseline implementations, evaluation scripts, starter kits, and submission instructions.

Participants will submit predictions, reasoning outputs, method descriptions, and reproducibility materials according to the requirements of each track.

Evaluation will use hidden test sets and standardized protocols. Track-specific metrics will evaluate prediction performance, ranking consistency, temporal reasoning, distortion attribution, and explanation quality as appropriate.

Participate via IEEE DataPort
